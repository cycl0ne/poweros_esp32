# Cobra

The Cobra Mk III of ELITE (1984), flying through space: past a planet and the
sun, stars behind it and dust going by. It weaves, banks into its turns and now
and then rolls right over, while the eye goes round it.

## Starting it

Type `Cobra` in a shell. It opens a screen of its own.
**Esc** or a click of the left mouse button ends it, and so does Ctrl-C from the
shell it was started in.

It draws through `facet.library`, on the GPU where the machine has one and on
the cores if not.

## In a shell

`Cobra FRAMES/K/N,CORES/S,WINDOW/S,WIRE/S`

- **FRAMES** ends it after so many frames and says how many were shown a second.
- **CORES** draws on the cores even where there is a GPU.
- **WINDOW** flies it in a window on the desktop, which can be moved and sized.
  Its close gadget or Esc ends it there.
- **WIRE** shows it as ELITE drew it: white lines, those behind hidden, with the
  stars and the dust alone.

## What is ELITE's

The ship's 28 corners, its 13 faces and its 38 lines are ELITE's numbers. The
colours, the light, the flames, the barrel of the gun and all the rest are made
here. The flight is not the game: nothing is steered or shot at.
